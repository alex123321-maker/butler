Riverside cultural centre — festival handover pack
Internal planning edition dated 18 October 2026
This is a fictional event with synthetic organisations and correspondence.

The programme spans a lecture room, a small theatre and an outdoor courtyard.
The venue booking covers the two indoor rooms. Courtyard access is provisional
pending the neighbouring library's agreement on the shared delivery lane. The
programme draft has placed a music workshop outdoors, but the room allocation
sheet still labels its location 'to be confirmed'. A programme item is not an
independent venue permission.

The equipment supplier's first quotation lists eight radio microphones and
three receivers. A revised quotation lists six microphones and six receivers,
with two wired microphones offered instead of the missing radio units. The
event coordinator has asked whether the revised set can support simultaneous
sessions in both rooms. No technician's channel plan is attached. Comparing
microphone totals alone does not establish compatible radio frequencies or
adequate receiver capacity for the actual programme.

The ticket system reports 186 reservations for the evening discussion. The
theatre has 180 numbered seats, including four positions that can be removed
for wheelchair access. Six reservations are staff allocations made before the
public sale opened. The box office has not yet confirmed which staff allocations
will be used. It has temporarily stopped sales while checking the list. That
action does not prove an over-capacity audience will actually attend, nor does
it authorize removing accessible positions from the plan.

Two speakers have approved short biographies. A third speaker's assistant sent
a revised title but did not approve the biography text. The publicity folder
marks the whole email thread as 'received'. The editor uses a separate column
for publication approval and has left the third biography blank there. The
revised title can be transcribed as correspondence without implying approval
of every earlier sentence in the thread.

The catering sheet distinguishes confirmed dietary requests from estimated
meal counts. There are fourteen recorded vegetarian requests, three nut-allergy
notices and seventy general lunch reservations. Some people appear in both a
dietary category and the general reservation count. The organiser has not
deduplicated those lists in this extract. Adding all three numbers would not
establish a total number of lunches. The caterer has asked for a reconciled
person count without unnecessary personal details.

A rehearsal on Thursday tested projection and amplified speech in the lecture
room. The theatre projector was unavailable because a school group was using
the room. A volunteer noted that the same laptop should probably work there.
That expectation has not been tested against the theatre's adapter and cable
run. The technical lead has reserved a short test on Tuesday and has kept the
two rooms' readiness columns separate in the handover.

The publicity schedule originally proposed sending the final visitor email on
19 October. The booking team now expects to finish checking staff tickets on
20 October. The programme editor has proposed a brief save-the-date message
followed by room details later. Neither proposal has been selected. A draft
email exists in the shared folder; its presence does not establish that it was
sent or that visitors have acknowledged changed room information.

An outdoor delivery is planned between 08:00 and 08:30. The courier confirmed
the proposed arrival range, while the library replied that its access meeting
would happen on Monday. No later lane agreement is included. The centre has a
fallback delivery route through its own rear entrance, but larger flight cases
will not fit through that door. The supplier has been asked to provide actual
case dimensions before the fallback can be called usable.

Volunteer rotas show twelve people for the morning and nine for the evening.
Five names appear in both shifts. Three morning volunteers have completed the
new evacuation briefing; the others attended an older general orientation.
The coordinator has scheduled a new briefing but has not taken attendance yet.
The number of names on a rota is consequently different from the number of
independently briefed people available for a particular safety assignment.

The handover's open decisions concern the courtyard agreement, the actual radio
channel plan, the reconciled ticket and meal lists, the third biography approval,
the theatre projection test and the final email schedule. These issues should
not be collapsed into a single 'festival ready' statement. A useful comparison
will distinguish a confirmed observation, a conditional arrangement, an estimate
and an unanswered request, while leaving operational decisions to their owners.
