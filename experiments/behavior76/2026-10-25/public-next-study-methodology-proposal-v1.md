# Prospective next study proposal; no native authorization

This proposal does not modify the closed A/B/C or fresh-v3 protocols, controls,
judgments, mappings, records or costs. Their failures, missingness and protocol
deviations remain part of the research history. Current cumulative accounting
is376 native admissions,2,867,576 measured token subtotal and4 unavailable-usage
attempts; the subtotal is not an invoice and unavailable usage is not zero.
All original mappings and masked quality remain undisclosed.

## Preferred path: retain strict global stop

First establish reproducible public infrastructure evidence. The unknown
synthetic outcome needs bounded durable recipient diagnostics, independent
before/after/receipt evidence, and a real-Broker failure matrix. If the fixture
has a transactional or response-contract defect, repair that defect identically
in both arms. Do not weaken production Broker/source/policy behavior or promote
an unknown tool outcome to success. The authority failures need phase-specific
scripted diagnostics and proof that their actual cancellation cause globally
seals admission. Expected input denials, blocked/waiting outcomes, queue-head
blocking, per-run cap exhaustion and global authority failure must be distinct.
Any legitimate waiting/blocked state must be exported promptly without being
resumed, rewritten or observed as running for an avoidable20min poll.

Required pre-native gates after the necessary fixture freeze:

1. Clean committed source plus exact collector/Broker/runtime/schema/provider
   contract identities; unchanged candidate addition and acceptance thresholds.
   Explicit diff review separates infrastructure diagnostics/observer behavior
   from task authority, source admission and model-facing behavior.
2. Public failure matrix above, including lost ACK versus proven rejection;
   ordinary queued runs preserve each task's mandate, all finals/effects and
   current-source proofs. Provider/auth/currentness/journal corruption tests
   prove a durable global seal before any next provider admission. Include
   terminal/waiting/blocked owner and automatic cohorts without fake completion.
3. Full zero-native setup/composition through actual Input/Core/Profile/Catalog
   and guarded Broker after every typed event and restart; exact deletion anchor
   backup and all consumer-proof tables; no credential/provider factory.
4. Exact all-history row equality, immutable predecessor inventories and cost
   accounting; model-operation reservation versus wrapper admission separated;
   successful measured tokens/version and every NULL retained independently.
5. Only after semantic fixture freeze, independently author24 substantively new
   synthetic episodes across the same12 positive/negative families, two owner
   opportunities each,48 arms/96 planned opportunities. Preserve the family
   boundaries implicated by old failures rather than choosing easier cases.
   No old started-case replay, new candidate selection, quality tuning or
   parameter/search resampling. Old goldens/judgments remain sealed unchanged.
6. Freeze new corpus/golden/scorer literal bindings, masked mapping/schedule,
   per-run/slot/window limits and absolute deadlines before entry. Re-run fresh
   setup/composition independently; pin the final producer/ELF and obtain a new
   explicit audited admission decision. This proposal supplies none.

Collect once, under the declared strict global stop. An unexpected ambiguous
effect may correctly terminate such a study. It cannot be promised to produce
all48 arms, nor may incompleteness be called a passing full pilot. Retain the
fixed96 denominator, distinguish infrastructure delivery failure from observed
language error, seal all masked judgments before mapping, and reject promotion
on critical UNKNOWN or missing required evidence.

## Alternative requiring a separate prospective amendment

Current production semantics conservatively classify even some deterministic
runtime failed reads as UNKNOWN after dispatch. If public diagnostics show that
this makes one model's ordinary negative boundary behavior abort unrelated
isolated fixtures, a separately declared per-isolated-fixture stop is a
methodologically defensible collection design. It is not a reinterpretation of
the closed protocols and does not make their continuation compliant.

Under that design, only a prospectively enumerated fixture-local tool outcome
may end its owning episode-arm and stop workers/admission for its entire actual task tree/cohort.
Retain the attempted call, UNKNOWN, failed/unavailable remainder and costs; do
not resume blocked work, seed the same arm again, replace the answer or infer
safe automatic retry. Start only the next originally scheduled independent
fixture. The other masked arm remains scheduled in its original order, not
reselected because one answer was inconvenient. Physical schemas, authority,
sessions, recipient objects and anchors must be independently isolated and a
public test must prove no cross-fixture grants, effects or queued workers survive.

Authentication/provider errors, source or ownership/currentness observer
unavailability, journal/counter/hash corruption, budget-window/deadline breach,
unclassified errors, isolation failure or inability to quiesce/seal a fixture
still stop globally. A model cannot choose a local-stop classification.
Task/branch Stop semantics and all production fail-closed kernel behavior stay
unchanged. Independently observed absent synthetic effects may be reported as
bounded recipient facts, but original Broker UNKNOWN stays UNKNOWN and can
never satisfy a critical acceptance criterion.

Such a study can finish the scheduled48 arm dispositions with failures. It is
not evidence of96 usable successful responses or a passed acceptance matrix.
Report completed scheduling, actual admitted owner turns, native finals, source
and effect evidence, paired semantic availability and costs separately. Include
every stopped/missing arm in intention-to-test denominators. No complete-case
filter, compensating extra cases, hidden suffix selection or repeated window
until a favorable result is allowed. A negative complete collection may be a
useful research result; implementation/whole-product closure still requires the
original issue criteria and their independent evidence.

The preferred path remains diagnosing/repairing a demonstrated fixture defect
and keeping strict global stop. The alternative requires its own public
operational rationale, independent review and freeze before fresh authorship;
it grants no new native call and does not alter candidate or quality thresholds.
