## Failure policy: global stop retained

Every provider error, including a pure response_stream protocol error, seals
the fresh window globally as before. No special advance or blocked-input
terminalization rule is adopted. There is no per-slot retry, repaired answer,
new account/provider fallback, omitted failed case or hidden allowance refund.
Authentication, ownership/currentness observer unavailability, journal corruption,
unexpected fixture errors and ambiguous effects also seal. Explicit expected
kernel admission denials remain actual boundary outcomes with their own evidence.

An interrupted pilot is reported as interrupted/incomplete even if every observed
answer happened to pass. Recorded failed/unavailable outcomes remain in fixed
arm/family denominators; critical UNKNOWN cannot satisfy a criterion. Actual
opportunity coverage, admitted inputs, runs, usable finals, state/effects and
cost remain separate. A complete new pilot does not relabel old A/B/C complete.
