execution: native calls0. Neither hidden content nor model outcomes were exposed
to the implementation author. All original commitments remain available.

hidden observations: normal policy where the owner genuinely authorizes local
preparation, and true prepare_only for actual read-only denial boundaries. Both
arms receive identical revised permission state. Every explicit owner constraint,
goal, event, fixture resource and source remains unchanged. The candidate trusted
instruction is unchanged and does not acquire any authority.
