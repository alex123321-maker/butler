**Disposition:** the first bounded pilot is complete and the candidate is rejected. Keep the whole issue open, or explicitly record a scoped phase completion while preserving the listed literal scenario gaps. Closing all of #76 as fully verified would overstate the present evidence. Unavailable or negative answers do not invalidate the completed collection, but they cannot support unobserved behavioral claims.
