The original r3 collection completed operationally with 24 synthetic episodes, 12 scenario families, 48 arms and all 96 planned owner opportunities assessed. This does not mean 96 successful answers. The frozen mechanical validator passed its evidence-admission procedure; the original quality and safety criteria reject promotion of the candidate. No production promotion or deployment is supported.

This is a small, synthetic, paired pilot with dependent turns and only two episodes per family. Communication assessment is independent AI review, supported by separate actual task/source/Broker/effect evidence, not human field validation. Invalid or incomplete canonical outputs remain unqualified. Two historical canonical replies had actually been privacy-redacted; they were not restored. Responses can themselves quote instruction snippets, so perfect response-based blinding cannot be guaranteed despite withholding arm labels and excluding operator instruction content from review projections.

The bounded first-pilot phase is complete with a useful negative conclusion. Literal closure of the whole current issue #76 is not yet supported by this pilot alone: all twelve family labels have positive/negative examples, but some narrower fixtures do not exercise the issue's exact ambiguity, conflict and feedback trajectories. The companion criterion audit identifies those limits. This is a coverage finding, not a requirement that a negative candidate become successful. No new experiment or inference is authorized or proposed here.
