# Issue97: prospective synthetic v2 protocol

Subsystems: native transport usage/profile configuration and an explicitly
disabled-by-default research command. The [command README](../../cmd/effort-eval/README.md)
defines exact arms, fixture boundaries, grading, reservations and invocation.

The owner authorized this PC only, unlimited use of the existing Gemini
subscription, then a new local browser login when the separately authorized
SFTP-only auth-file copy failed. No code, model or test ran on laba. The new
login persisted directly into an encrypted, independent local PostgreSQL store.
Neither Google account identity nor token material belongs in committed evidence.

Before quality inference:

1. Complete disjoint protocol preflights and offline/native-wire/PG tests.
2. Commit the implementation, cases, rubric, prompt, native schema and this
   protocol; rebuild from that commit. Record commit and binary SHA256 in Source.
3. Freeze all96 quality slots and their limits in PostgreSQL and a new export
   directory. Do not modify/retry them after observing quality outputs.
4. Execute every declared slot in its frozen rotated order. Retain failures and
   attempt usage without replacing them. Provider failures count against observed
   completion, separately from semantic mistakes.
5. Export the full immutable input manifest, state snapshots, outcomes and attempts.
   Produce per-case results, p50/p95, call/read counts, known total/input/output/
   thinking and UNKNOWN denominators. No subscription cash/quota saving inference.
6. Review free text separately against source fixtures and report unsupported
   assertions/questions. Compute paired arm differences descriptively; one
   repetition and a deliberately small corpus cannot establish equivalence.

The four modes separate a generic repeat, a checklist on existing evidence and
the same checklist with new evidence permitted. Record actual evidence delivery;
no delivered evidence means its incremental benefit is unmeasured. Differences
between independent model samples are not proof that a prompt alone caused them.

Decisions permitted by this pilot: keep current High; consider further Low testing
in explicitly identified slices; reject extra passes for cheap/simple tasks; or
insufficient evidence. No automatic production switch is part of #97. This is a
fixture-based feasibility result, not a real-mail or ordinary Core/Broker test.
